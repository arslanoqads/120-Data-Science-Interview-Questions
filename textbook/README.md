# AI Engineer / FDE Curriculum — Textbook

Compiled course chapters synthesized from `research/` notes. **Research is the only source of truth**; chapters do not invent capabilities beyond those notes.

## Compilation rule

Compile **one week at a time**. A week is marked `COMPLETE` only when its chapter covers every syllabus concept with the full concept structure. Do not start week *N+1* until week *N* is COMPLETE.

## Chapter concept structure

Each concept uses:

1. **Fundamentals**
2. **The Alternatives**
3. **Failure Modes**
4. **Average vs. Strong Engineer**
5. **Worked Example** (tied to the week’s Build)
6. **Apply It**

If research lacks grounding for a section: `[NEEDS MORE RESEARCH]`.
