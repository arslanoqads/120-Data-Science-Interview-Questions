# Surah 37: As-Saffat

**Volume(s):** 8  
**PDF pages:** 4817–4889 (73 pages)  
**Approximate length:** 17,356 words

---

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah As-Saffat** (Chapter 37 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Paradise and Hell
- Prophethood and Revelation
- Angels and Jinn
- Covenant and Obedience
- Prayer and Worship
- Stories of Past Nations
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Family and Social Law
- Day of Judgment
- Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
- Guidance and the Straight Path
- Mercy and Forgiveness
