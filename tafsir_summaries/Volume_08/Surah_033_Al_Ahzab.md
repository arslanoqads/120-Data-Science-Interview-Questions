**Volume(s):** 7, 8  
**PDF pages:** 4495–4646 (152 pages)

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Ahzab** (Chapter 33 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Prayer and Worship
- Covenant and Obedience
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Family and Social Law
- Paradise and Hell
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Stories of Past Nations
- Day of Judgment
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Angels and Jinn
- Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
