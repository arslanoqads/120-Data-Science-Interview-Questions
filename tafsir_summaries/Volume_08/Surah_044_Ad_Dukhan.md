**Volume(s):** 8, 9  
**PDF pages:** 5253–5282 (30 pages)

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Ad-Dukhan** (Chapter 44 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Paradise and Hell
- Prophethood and Revelation
- Stories of Past Nations
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Family and Social Law
- Day of Judgment
- Covenant and Obedience
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Angels and Jinn
- Guidance and the Straight Path
- Prayer and Worship
