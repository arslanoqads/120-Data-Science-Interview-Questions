## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Fussilat** (Chapter 41 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Paradise and Hell

- Prayer and Worship
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Day of Judgment

- Stories of Past Nations
