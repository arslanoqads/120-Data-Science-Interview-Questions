## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Fatihah** (Chapter 1 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Prayer and Worship
- Angels and Jinn
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Guidance and the Straight Path
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Paradise and Hell
- Covenant and Obedience
- Day of Judgment
- Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
- Family and Social Law
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief

Satan so that he is prevented from affecting my religious or
worldly affairs, or hindering me from adhering to what I was
commanded, or luring me into what I was prohibited from.”

touching the son of Adam. This is why Allah allowed us to be
lenient and kind with the human devil, so that his soft nature
