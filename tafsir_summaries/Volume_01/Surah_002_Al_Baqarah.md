## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Baqarah** (Chapter 2 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Family and Social Law
- Covenant and Obedience
- Prayer and Worship
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Paradise and Hell
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Angels and Jinn
- Stories of Past Nations
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Day of Judgment
- Guidance and the Straight Path
