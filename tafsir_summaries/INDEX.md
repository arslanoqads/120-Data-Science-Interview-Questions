| Volume | Surahs Covered |
|--------|----------------|
| Volume 1 | 1 (Al-Fatihah), 2 (partial Al-Baqarah) |
| Volume 2 | 2 (Al-Baqarah continued), 3 (Al-Imran), 4 (partial An-Nisa) |
| Volume 3 | 4 (An-Nisa continued), 5 (Al-Maidah), 6 (Al-Anam) |
| Volume 4 | 7–10 (Al-Araf through Yunus) |
| Volume 5 | 11–17 (Hud through partial Al-Isra) |
| Volume 6 | 16–23 (An-Nahl continued through Al-Muminun) |

| Volume 10 | 64–114 (At-Taghabun through An-Nas) |
