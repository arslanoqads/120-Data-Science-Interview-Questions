**Volume(s):** 6  
**PDF pages:** 3681–3792 (112 pages)

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Hajj** (Chapter 22 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Paradise and Hell
- Prayer and Worship
- Stories of Past Nations
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Covenant and Obedience
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Day of Judgment
- Angels and Jinn
- Guidance and the Straight Path
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
