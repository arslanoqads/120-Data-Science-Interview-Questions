## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Haqqah** (Chapter 69 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Stories of Past Nations
- Angels and Jinn
- Paradise and Hell
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Covenant and Obedience
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Prayer and Worship
- Knowledge and Wisdom
