**Volume(s):** 10  
**PDF pages:** 6003–6023 (21 pages)

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah At-Tahrim** (Chapter 66 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Family and Social Law
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Covenant and Obedience
- Paradise and Hell
- Angels and Jinn
- Prayer and Worship
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Day of Judgment
- Stories of Past Nations
