Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

## Detailed Section Summaries

### 1. the Tafsir of Surat Al-Balad (see 90:20)

Then
Allah says,
{In pillars stretchedforth.}
“Atiyah Al-‘Awfi said, “Pillars of Iron.” As-Suddi said, “Made of
fire.” Al-‘Awfi reported from Ibn ‘Abbas, “He will make them
enter pillars stretched forth, meaning there will be columns
over them, and they will have chains on their necks, and the
gates (of Hell) will be shut upon them.”|2,
This is the end of the Tafsir of Surat Al-Humazah, and all
praise and thanks are due to Allah.
