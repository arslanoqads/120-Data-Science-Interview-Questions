## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah An-Nas** (Chapter 114 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Angels and Jinn
- Stories of Past Nations
- Family and Social Law
- Prayer and Worship
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Covenant and Obedience
- Paradise and Hell
- Justice and Rights
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
- Patience and Trust in Allah
