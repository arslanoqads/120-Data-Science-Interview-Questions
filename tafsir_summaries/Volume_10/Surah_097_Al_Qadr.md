## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Qadr** (Chapter 97 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Angels and Jinn
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Prayer and Worship
