## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Ghashiyah** (Chapter 88 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Paradise and Hell
- Stories of Past Nations
- Covenant and Obedience
- Prayer and Worship
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief

### 13. how it has been spread out, extended and made

Thus, He directs the bedouin to consider what he
himself witnesses. His camel that he rides upon, the sky that
is above his head, the mountain that faces him, and the earth
that is under him, all of this is proof of the power of the
Creator and Maker of these things. These things should lead
him to see that He is the Lord, the Most Great, the Creator,
the Owner, and the Controller of everything. Therefore, He is
the God other than Whom none deserves to be worshipped.
