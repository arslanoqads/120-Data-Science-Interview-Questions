## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Al-Maarij** (Chapter 70 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Paradise and Hell
- Prayer and Worship
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Prophethood and Revelation
- Family and Social Law
- Covenant and Obedience
- Stories of Past Nations
- Angels and Jinn
- Patience and Trust in Allah
- Day of Judgment
