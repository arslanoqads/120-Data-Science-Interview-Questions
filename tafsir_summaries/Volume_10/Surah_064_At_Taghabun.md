**Volume(s):** 10  
**PDF pages:** 5963–5977 (15 pages)

## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah At-Taghabun** (Chapter 64 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

---

## Main Themes and Key Concepts

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Hypocrisy and Disbelief
- Family and Social Law
- Mercy and Forgiveness
- Paradise and Hell
- Stories of Past Nations
- Covenant and Obedience
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Guidance and the Straight Path
- Day of Judgment
