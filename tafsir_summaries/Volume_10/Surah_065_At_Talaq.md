## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah At-Talaq** (Chapter 65 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

- Prophethood and Revelation
- Covenant and Obedience
- Knowledge and Wisdom
- Paradise and Hell

- Day of Judgment
- Patience and Trust in Allah
- Mercy and Forgiveness
