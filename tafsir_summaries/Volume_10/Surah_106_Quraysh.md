## Overview

This document summarizes the abridged **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** (Darussalam English edition) for **Surah Quraysh** (Chapter 106 of the Qur'an). Ibn Kathir explains verse meanings, cites authentic hadiths from Prophet Muhammad, records views of the Companions and early scholars, and provides historical and legislative context.

- Prayer and Worship
- Covenant and Obedience
- Prophethood and Revelation
- Stories of Past Nations
- Mercy and Forgiveness
