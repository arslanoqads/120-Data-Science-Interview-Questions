# Tafsir Ibn Kathir — Modular Audited Edition

**Source:** Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Abridged), Darussalam English Edition

Audited for redundant hadith narrations, isnad chains removed, Arabic/non-English text stripped. Organized into modules (~50 pages) and volumes (max 10 modules each).

- **Modules:** 36
- **Volumes:** 4
